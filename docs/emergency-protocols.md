# Emergency protocols (summary)
- **Numbers:** Police 999 | Civil Defence 997 | Ambulance 998.
- **Critical banner:** stop, move indoors/interior, verify with official channels, check on neighbours.
- **Industrial/chemical (Jebel Ali):** seal room, shut AC, move upwind/crosswind or to a high floor if gas is heavier than air.
- **Explosion/air raid:** shelter in an interior room away from glass, below top floors if possible; stay 30+ minutes unless told otherwise.
- **High-rise evacuation:** use stairs, never lifts; know two exits; assemble at the designated point.
